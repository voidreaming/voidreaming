# Hi, I'm Shouju Wang

I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, advised by Prof. Haopeng Zhang.

My research focuses on building trustworthy AI agents, especially **LLM privacy**, **contextual integrity**, **agent alignment**, and **multimodal agent evaluation**. Previously, I worked on privacy for LLM agents at Microsoft Research Asia and on fair graph anomaly detection at Duke Kunshan University.

## Selected research

- **[MPCI-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08235)** — a benchmark for multimodal pairwise contextual integrity evaluation of language-model agents.
- **[Privacy in Action](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.925/)** — realistic privacy mitigation and evaluation for LLM-powered agents, published in Findings of EMNLP 2025.
- **[DECAF-GAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10785)** — fairness-aware autoencoders for node-level graph anomaly detection, presented at ECAI 2025.

## What I'm working on

- Privacy and contextual integrity for multimodal agents
- Agent safety, alignment, and realistic evaluation
- Efficient inference and post-training systems for large models
